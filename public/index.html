Why this matters

Reaching net-zero runs through our value chain

71% of The Corporate's carbon footprint sits in our value chain — in the goods and services we buy, not inside our own plants. That makes our suppliers the single biggest lever we have to reach net-zero by 2045. We can't get there without you.

This programme gives us a clear, reliable picture of how our suppliers are performing on sustainability, and a practical way to act on it together. It's also how we meet our growing sustainability reporting obligations — we can't report what we can't see.

Just as important: this is the start of a working relationship, not a one-off audit. Strong performers earn preferential standing, and we partner with suppliers to improve over time.

Across the programme we focus on six shared priorities:

  • Climate
  • Pollution
  • Water
  • Circular economy
  • People
  • Governance

How it works

One programme. One assessment. No wasted effort.

We call it Smart Intake. Long, repetitive questionnaires sent to everyone produce fatigue and unreliable data — so we've built the programme around a simple efficiency rule.

We recognise the work you've already done. A valid EcoVadis scorecard already demonstrates your performance, so those suppliers are exempt from our full assessment — just share it. Everyone else completes one single, well-structured assessment. Once, clearly.

Recognise existing standards

What you do now

Answer one question to find your path

Do you hold a valid EcoVadis scorecard issued within the last 12 months?

Path A If you have a valid scorecard

Share your EcoVadis scorecard

Submit or share your current EcoVadis scorecard with your Account Manager. A valid scorecard satisfies the assessment in full — you don't need to complete the questionnaire. Validations are processed on a rolling basis.
